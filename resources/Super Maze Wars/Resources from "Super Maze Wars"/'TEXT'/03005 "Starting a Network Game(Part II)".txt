• Type the number of robot opponents you wish to compete with.• Choose a maze.• Choose one of the game scenarios.• Choose a timed game or a game up to a number of points or rounds.(When you change any of the options, all players see the change.)• Click “Ready”.• You may have to wait while other players join the game. Players who are ready have check marks next to their scoreboard names. The game starts when all players are ready.• That’s it! Read on to learn how to play and win.Note: You can’t start a network game until at least one person joins you.
