On the radar, gems and foxes are shown as rectangles, the gems in yellow, and foxes according to their scoreboard colour. The gems and foxes are located somewhere within the rectangle, but you must use the View Screen to find out exactly where. In a gem-collection scenario, the gem rectangle’s size is proportional to the number of gems you have collected in the current round. In Fox & Hounds, the fox rectangle’s size is inversely proportional to the number of gems the fox has collected in the current round. Therefore, as rounds go on, gems get progressively harder to find, and foxes get progressively easier to find. Hounds in a Fox & Hounds game cannot see the gem.
