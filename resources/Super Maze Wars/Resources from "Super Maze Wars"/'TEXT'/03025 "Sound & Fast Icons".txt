• Sound Volume Indicator. Press the minus ‘-’ key to lower the sound volume and the plus ‘+’ key to raise it.• “Fast” Capability Indicators. Show which “fast” capabilities you have acquired by picking up capsules. From left to right, they are fast blasts, fast MazeCruiser speed, and fast MazeCruiser turns.• Progress Display. Shows the time remaining (for timed games) or the goal of the game (for games up to a number of points or rounds).
