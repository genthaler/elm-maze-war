Capsules are rotating cuboids that appear in the maze. You can pick up a capsule by driving through it. Each capsule contains an enhancement to your MazeCruiser and is colored accordingly. You cannot kill capsules. A capsule can add to your:“Weapon” Capabilities:• Increase the reserve power of your shield• Increase the number of blasts in your magazine• Increase the number of missiles in your magazine“Fast” Capabilities: (shown along the bottom of your dashboard)• Increase the speed of your blasts• Increase the speed at which your MazeCruiser moves• Increase the speed at which your MazeCruiser turns
